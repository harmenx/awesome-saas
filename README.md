## Productivity & Project Management

- [Notion](https://www.notion.com) - All-in-one workspace for notes, docs, and project management.
- [ClickUp](https://clickup.com) - Project management with flexible workflows.
- [Trello](https://trello.com) - Visual task management for teams.
- [Airtable](https://www.airtable.com) - Spreadsheet-database hybrid for organizing projects.

## Marketing & Growth

- [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com) - CRM, marketing automation, and sales platform.
- [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com) - Email marketing and automation.
- [ConvertKit](https://kit.com) - Email marketing for creators.
- [GetResponse](https://www.getresponse.com) - Email, landing pages, webinars.
- [Buffer](https://buffer.com) - Social media scheduling and analytics.
- [OpenSparta](https://opensparta.com/en) - Telegram marketing workspace for teams and agencies to manage connected accounts and proxies and discover relevant conversations.

## Development & IT

- [GitHub](https://github.com) - Code hosting, CI/CD, and collaboration.
- [GitLab](https://gitlab.com) - DevOps lifecycle management.
- [Render](https://render.com) - Cloud hosting for apps and websites.
- [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com) - Hosting for static sites with CI/CD.
- [Postman](https://www.postman.com) - API development and testing.

## Finance & Accounting

- [QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com) - Accounting and bookkeeping software.
- [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com) - Cloud-based invoicing and accounting.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com) - Payment processing and subscriptions.
- [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com) - SaaS payments, subscriptions, and billing.
